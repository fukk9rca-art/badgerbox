the badgerbox extension

open an assignment on google classroom, or a youtube video, click the badger, and it sends the assignment, its files and the transcript here in one go.
  1. open your browser's extensions page: opera://extensions, or chrome://extensions in chrome.
  2. turn on developer mode.
  3. press "load unpacked" and pick the extension folder inside your badgerbox folder.
  4. pin the badger, then click it on an assignment page.
